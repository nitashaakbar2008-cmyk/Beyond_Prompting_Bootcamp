# Lumière Books: approved store information

Source: the existing storefront pages (src/storefront/views.js: FAQ, Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms) and footer contact details. Policy text copied from those pages; the AI-assistant privacy note and the "Not available" list are new clarifications. This is a DEMONSTRATION store.

## About
- Demo storefront. The catalogue is fictional demonstration data, not a publisher-authorised listing. No real payments or deliveries occur.
- Prices are in Pakistani rupees (PKR).

## How to order
- Add books to the cart, then check out. Payment method: Cash on Delivery (demo simulation). Do not enter card or banking details.
- Checkout asks for full name, email, phone number, delivery address, city, postal code (order notes optional).
- A confirmation page with an order ID is shown; the initial order status is "Pending".

## Delivery (shipping information)
- Demo delivery charge: PKR 250 per order, free on orders of PKR 5,000 or more.
- Estimated delivery: 3–5 working days (illustrative; no real shipments).

## Returns and refunds
- Demo policy: damaged or incorrect books may be returned within 7 days of delivery for replacement or refund. No actual refunds are processed in this prototype.

## Privacy and data
- The existing storefront stores the cart, orders, messages and newsletter email in the visitor's own browser. If a visitor uses the Lumi AI assistant, the messages they type are sent to the store's server and an AI service to generate replies; visitors should not share personal details in the chat.

## Terms
- Non-commercial demonstration. Catalogue titles, authors, ISBNs and publishers are fictional.
